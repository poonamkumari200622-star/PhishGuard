Link awareness
Think before you click
The internet is full of links. Not all are safe.

A single misleading link can expose personal information. Awareness is your first layer of protection.

THE PROBLEM

A familiar-looking link can hide a real threat.

Phishing attempts often imitate trusted websites and use urgency, rewards or fear to influence people.

01 / IMPERSONATION
◈

Fake websites

Fraudsters create websites that look similar to banks, shopping platforms and trusted services.

02 / URGENCY
⌛

Urgent messages

Messages may threaten account suspension or promise rewards to make people act quickly.

03 / MISLEADING LINKS
↗

Suspicious URLs

Slight changes in spelling, unusual domains and misleading website addresses can be warning signs.

Example: paytm-secure.example
