THE DIGITAL THREAT

One careless click can change everything.

Online fraud often begins with a message, a misleading link or a request that creates unnecessary urgency.

01 / THREAT
📩

Fake Messages

Scammers send messages pretending to be banks, delivery services or trusted organisations.

02 / THREAT
🔗

Suspicious Links

Fraudulent websites may imitate genuine websites to collect personal or financial information.

03 / THREAT
⚠️

Urgent Requests

Fake warnings and reward claims pressure people into making unsafe decisions quickly.

SMS ALERT ⚠ SUSPICIOUS
UNKNOWN SENDER

Your bank account will be blocked today! Verify your KYC immediately using this link: fake-bank-verify.example

⚠ Warning: Do not open unexpected banking links. Visit your bank's official website instead.
UPI FRAUD ⚠ SUSPICIOUS
UNKNOWN CALLER

You have received ₹5,000! Enter your UPI PIN to receive the money immediately.

⚠ Warning: Never enter your UPI PIN to receive money.
REWARD SCAM ⚠ SUSPICIOUS
FAKE REWARD TEAM

Congratulations! You won a special prize. Pay a small processing fee to claim your reward.

⚠ Warning: Unexpected prizes demanding payment may be fraudulent.
DELIVERY SCAM ⚠ SUSPICIOUS
FAKE DELIVERY SERVICE

Your parcel is on hold. Update your delivery address immediately through this suspicious link.

⚠ Warning: Check delivery updates through the official delivery app or website.
